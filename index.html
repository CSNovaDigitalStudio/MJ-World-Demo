WHO WE ARE

A central connection point for the logistics industry.

MJ World is a logistics support institution connecting people, businesses, service providers and opportunities across the logistics industry and Southern Africa.

We do not own a fleet and we are not a freight carrier. Our purpose is to connect the right people, services and support around the industry so businesses and professionals can move forward with greater confidence.

Our position: MJ World is not hiring — we connect talent to the industry.
